Hoku Gmail Integration

Use information

A personal integration maintained for its owner.

Purpose and access

Hoku Gmail Integration connects Eva, the owner's personal assistant, to the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar using explicit Google authorization. This website does not provide a public assistant service, offer subscriptions or accept account registrations.

Google's services remain subject to the owner's agreements with Google. Google authorization can be withdrawn through the owner's Google Account. An expired or revoked grant requires the owner to reconnect before the assistant can resume API access.

Data practices

The privacy policy describes what the integration accesses and how information is used and retained. The maintainer is keonilamonte-eng.
