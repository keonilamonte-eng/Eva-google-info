Hoku Gmail Integration

Privacy policy

Google data is used to support the owner's personal assistant workflows.

Google information accessed

After explicit Google authorization, Eva can read and search Gmail messages, read Google Calendar information, and manage events on calendars the owner owns. The integration requests Gmail read-only, Calendar read-only and owned-calendar event permissions. It does not request permission to send or modify Gmail messages, manage Calendar sharing, or administer the Google account.

How information is used

Message and event information supports the owner's requests, personal summaries, scheduling and assistant context. Google user data is not used for advertising or sold. The Google integration is not a service for collecting other people's Google accounts.

The current primary assistant model runs on the owner's computer. Google API requests necessarily communicate with Google. If the owner enables other connected features or changes processing providers, information supplied to those features may be processed by the selected service under that service's terms and privacy practices.

Storage and access

Google authorization credentials are kept in private local files restricted to the owner's Windows account and SYSTEM. Refresh tokens allow ordinary access-token renewal without another sign-in. Assistant history, cached information, summaries and backups can contain Google-derived information and are retained locally under the owner's control.

Connection monitoring records service status, timestamps and fixed error categories. Its probes do not retain email bodies or event contents. Credentials and private Google data are not published with this information website.

Retention, deletion and revocation

The owner controls removal of retained local data and backups. Removing Google authorization does not automatically erase local history, summaries or backups. The owner can revoke the integration through the Google Account connections settings. Revocation prevents further authorized Google API access until the owner grants permission again.

Deleting local assistant copies does not itself delete messages or events in the Google account. Calendar changes explicitly requested through the assistant can change events in Google Calendar.

Questions and updates

The maintainer can be reached through the keonilamonte-eng GitHub profile. This policy should be updated if the integration's permissions or data practices change.
